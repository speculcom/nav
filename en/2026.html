AI directory · 2026 additions · 2026-11-18

2026 new doors: official pages first, leaderboards second

This is not a list padded to a count; it picks official doors worth re-adding in 2026: a few models, compute pages and protocols, with why they matter now and what they do not guarantee.

The directory’s job is to send you elsewhere. All entries point to official pages; whether an entry is already listed and its reachability status is shown on the directory entry page.
